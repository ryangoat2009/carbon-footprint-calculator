OUR PLANET. OUR RESPONSIBILITY.

Climate change is real!!

Our everyday choices leave a mark on the planet. The energy we use at home, the journeys we take, the food we eat, and the things we buy all contribute to our carbon footprint.

Understanding that footprint is a first step toward making thoughtful choices and reducing our environmental impact.

How big is your carbon footprint?

Take a closer look at your daily habits. Small changes, such as saving electricity, sharing a ride, reducing food waste, and reusing what you own, can help us care for the environment.
