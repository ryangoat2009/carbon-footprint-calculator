How this classroom estimate works

These are illustrative assumptions for a school programming project, not official emissions factors or a complete carbon inventory. Actual impact varies by country, product, fuel, and lifestyle. Country and age do not change the estimate.

  • Home: monthly kWh × 12 × 0.5 ÷ household size.
  • Travel: weekly car km × 52 × 0.2 ÷ passengers, plus annual flight hours × 90.
  • Food: 2, 3, 5, or 7 kg per day for plant-based, vegetarian, mixed, or meat-heavy diets, multiplied by 365.
  • Electronics: new devices per year × 70 kg.
  • Shopping: new clothing items per year × 10 kg.

All category results are in kg CO₂e per year. Divide the sum by 1,000 to display tonnes. The model excludes activities such as public transport, home heating fuels, and public services.
